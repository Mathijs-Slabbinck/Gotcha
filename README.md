# Gotcha

## Project Description
### What is Gotcha?
Gotcha (also known as Assassin) is a social elimination game that's all about stealth, creativity, and timing.
Everyone gets assigned a secret target from the player list.
Your goal? "Eliminate" your target by completing your chosen style of challenge.
It could be getting them to do something, or by catching them with a specific item.
(This is customizable per game).
Once you succeed, your target is out, and you take on their target.
The game continues until only one player remains!

### What the app offers:
Gotcha is a great game to play with big groups, but setting it up can be quite a hassle.
This app will remove the hassle of printing names and weapons and assigning them and will do it for you.
Working with traditional paper is also quite limited.
This app allows you to control what info people see to make it easier.
(For example, you can turn on player images for groups where not everyone may know each other that well).
The app also offers custom game modes that aren't possible with traditional paper.

### Custom Game Settings:
#### Free Settings:
- Show Player Images (show the images of all players that have one)
- Force Player Images (force every player to have an image uploaded to show them during the game)
- Show Real Names (Allows the hunter to see the targets name to identify them)
- Show Usernames (In case you play with friends and have nicknames for each other, or a scouts and you wanna use totem names, or ...)
- Custom Rules (In case the players have their own rules they have verbally agreed on. They will able to write them down so it's stored and there won't be any chance for avoidable discussions later on)
- Custom Confirmation Time (The time it takes to confirm the kill. If any other living player witnessed the kill they have this amount of time to void the kill before it gets confirmed)

#### Paid Settings:
- Assassin GameMode (you can kill your killer and the game will auto update the cycle, if player guessed wrong and killed the wrong killer they die in stead)
- Timed Kills (give players a time limit to kill their target. If the timer ends before they get their kill, they die).
- Chaos GameMode (Swaps the targets around every x amount of time (x is custimizable by the admin before the game starts, minimum is 0.5 days, max is 1 week))
- Custom Kill Methods (normal gotcha is played with 1 'weapon' (like hanging a clothespin on someone for 5s). This settings allows admins to add custom weapons (this can be for example: "book, cola can, beer opener" and you'd have to touch em for a given amount of time with that object, but this could also be tasks like "make them give you a compliment". If this settings is turned on the custom weapons will be distributed randomly)

### Notes:
- Admins will be able to "review" player profiles before the game starts. They will be able to do things like force players to change their image (in case it's a bad or random image and doesn't show them) or to change their username.
- Show Real Names & Usernames can both be on at the same time, they can't both be off at the same time.

### Notifications
- Target changed during Assassin GameMode due to your target being killed by it's target.
- Target changed during chaos GameMode.
- Kill timer reminder during IsTimed GameMode.

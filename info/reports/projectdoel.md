# Project Goals - MDE

## Description

### What is Gotcha?
Gotcha (also known as Assassin) is a social elimination game that’s all about stealth, creativity, and timing. Everyone gets assigned a secret target from the player list. Your goal? “Eliminate” your target by completing your chosen style of challenge. It could be getting them to do something, or by catching them with a specific item. (This is customizable per game). Once you succeed, your target is out, and you take on their target. The game continues until only one player remains!

### What the app offers:
Gotcha is a great game to play with big groups, but setting it up can be quite a hassle. This app will remove the hassle of printing names and weapons and assigning them and will do it for you. Working with traditional paper is also quite limited. This app allows you to control what info people see to make it easier. (For example, you can turn on player images for groups where not everyone may know each other that well). The app also offers custom game modes that aren't possible with traditional paper.

### FAQ:
- **What is the goal of the app?:**
  Making the process of setting up the popular social game Gotcha a lot easier while providing quality of life features (f.e. images for groups where not everyone may know each other that well) and settings that wouldn't be possible normally (f.e. assassing mode)
- **Which problem does the app solve?:**
  Setting up Gotcha is a hassle, this app makes it a breeze while offering extra functionality that isn't possible without it.
- **Who will use the app?:**
  Big groups of people that want to play the social game Gotcha. I have personally played this in scouts and school but even companies that want to do something fun in between could use it. If you have a large group that is together a lot for a while you are set to go.
- **How will the app be used?:**
  Mainly to keep track of the game states and handle the Gotcha games.

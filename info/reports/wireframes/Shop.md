# Shop

The shop page is for the User (when logged in but not in game)

Here they can buy the next settings permanently:
- Assassin
- Chaos
- Timed Kills
- Different Kill Methods
- Increase max lobby size to: (standard: 50)
  - 100 people
  - 150 people
  - 500 people
  - 10000 people

And the following plans (montly / weekly subscription):
- Standard [free]
- Premium (all settings unlocked, max lobby 50 people)
- Deluxe (all settings unlocked, max lobby 10000 people)

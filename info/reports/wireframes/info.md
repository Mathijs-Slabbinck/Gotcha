# Info

The info page is for unauthenticated users to learn about Gotcha.

**The page has 2 sections:**
- "What is Gotcha?" (explains the game concept: social elimination game, secret targets, eliminating targets by completing challenges, taking on their target after a successful kill, last one standing wins)
- "What the app offers" (explains why the app exists: removes the hassle of manual setup, allows custom settings like player images, and offers game modes not possible with traditional paper)
